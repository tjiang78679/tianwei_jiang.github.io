About

I am a Ph.D. student in ECE at Georgia Tech. I received my B.S. in Audio and Music Engineering and B.A. in Mathematics from the University of Rochester.

My research interests include audio perception, sound event detection, sound source localization and spatial audio, audio-visual learning, and multimodal machine learning. I am especially interested in building audio-centered systems that work reliably in real-world settings.

Outside research, I love K-pop and play piano, classical guitar, and drums. I also enjoy badminton, tennis, and skiing.
